# task 1.3 - computer systems around you

| computer based system | main purpose | one input | one output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| automated teller machine (atm) | dispense cash and show bank account balance | pin entered on keypad | cash dispensed from cash slot |
| traffic light control system | direct traffic at road intersection | road vehicle sensor or timer | traffic signal lights red yellow green |
| supermarket self checkout | scan item barcodes and process payments | barcode scanned by optical scanner | updated on screen total bill |
